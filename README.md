# Space for Young Explorers

**Open the site:** https://mehri56998.github.io/space-for-young-explorers/

A project for the **NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026** (Dushanbe), challenge
*Abandoned but not Forgotten: Storytelling about NASA's Discarded Equipment on the Moon and Mars*.

## What it is

An interactive website for explorers aged 8 to 12 about the machines NASA has left on the Moon,
on Mars and far beyond the planets: where they really are, what they did and what they taught us.

Inside the site:

- 24 cards about real spacecraft and equipment, each with a link to its NASA source
- a comic told by the spacecraft themselves, a quiz, a mission timeline, planets and a glossary
- browser games: Lunar Frontier, Shards of Pluto 3D and Ash of Orbit, each with a NASA Field Guide (press G)

## How to run

Open the link above. To run it offline, download `index.html` and open it in a browser.
The whole site is one file; an internet connection is needed only for the NASA photos and web fonts.

## NASA data and resources

- NASA Science mission pages (science.nasa.gov) for facts, dates and mission status
- NASA Space Science Data Coordinated Archive (NSSDCA) Master Catalog for spacecraft records
- NASA Image and Video Library (images.nasa.gov) for photographs
- NASA 3D Resources for the Voyager model used in Shards of Pluto 3D

The full list of sources, with links, is in the **Sources** section of the site.

## Use of AI

AI tools were used in making this project. This is described in the **Sources** section of the site,
and AI-generated pictures carry a visible "AI-generated" label. The comic and the game stories are fiction;
the spacecraft facts come from the NASA sources listed above.
